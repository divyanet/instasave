Facebook downloader FAQs

How do I download a Facebook video?
Copy the video's link from Facebook (Share → Copy Link), paste it into the box above and tap Download. The MP4 saves to your device in seconds — no login needed.
Can I download Facebook Reels?
Yes. Paste a Facebook Reel link the same way — our extractor handles reels, Watch videos and regular video posts from public sources.
Can I download videos from private Facebook groups?
No — and neither can any legitimate tool. Private groups, friends-only posts and DMs require a login. Only public videos can be downloaded.
What quality are Facebook video downloads?
We grab the best-quality MP4 Facebook serves for the video — typically HD — with the original audio intact.
Is it legal to download Facebook videos?
Keep downloads for personal, offline viewing and only save videos you own or have permission to keep. Don't re-upload someone else's content as your own.
Can I download Facebook Live streams?
Finished live streams that remain published as public videos can usually be downloaded. Streams in progress can't — wait until the stream ends and the replay is published.
Do I need a Facebook account to use this?
No. InstaSave only fetches public videos and never asks for your Facebook login — your account is never at risk.
