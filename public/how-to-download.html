How to download Instagram reels & photos

Three steps on any device. No app install, no login, no watermark.

The 3 steps (every device)

  1. Copy the link. In the Instagram app, tap Share → Copy link on the reel or post. On a computer, just copy the page URL from the address bar.
  2. Paste it into InstaSave. Open instasave.example.com, drop the link in the box, hit Download.
  3. Save the file. Preview the media, then tap the download button for the MP4 or JPG.

📱 On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Copy the reel/post link from the Instagram app.
  2. Open Safari and go to InstaSave. (Tip: tap the box and your copied link is offered automatically.)
  3. Paste, hit Download, then tap the file's download button.
  4. Safari shows a download icon in the address bar — tap it, then the file, then Share → Save Video / Save Image to move it into Photos. Or find it anytime in the Files app under Downloads.

🤖 On Android (Chrome)

  1. Copy the link from Instagram (Share → Copy link).
  2. Open Chrome, visit InstaSave, paste and hit Download.
  3. Tap the download button — Chrome saves it straight to your Downloads folder and shows a notification. Open it from there or your Gallery.

💻 On PC / Mac

  1. Copy the post URL from your browser's address bar while viewing the reel or photo.
  2. Paste it into InstaSave and hit Download.
  3. Click the download button — the MP4/JPG lands in your browser's download folder. Rename it whatever you like.

Troubleshooting

  • "Could not find media": the post is private, deleted, or the link has a typo. Only public posts work.
  • Video button missing on a reel: Instagram is temporarily blocking server fetches. You'll still get the cover photo — try again in a few minutes and the MP4 usually unlocks.
  • Download won't start on iPhone: make sure you're using Safari (not the in-app Instagram browser). Long-press the download button and choose Download Linked File.
  • Where did my file go? iPhone: Files app → Downloads (or Photos after saving). Android: Downloads folder / notification shade. PC: browser's download bar.

Quick tips

  • For carousels, each photo gets its own download button — grab only the ones you want.
  • Downloads are watermark-free originals — much sharper than screen recordings.
  • Keep downloads for personal offline use and credit creators if you ever share their work.
