About InstaSave

What is InstaSave?

InstaSave is a free, fast online tool that helps you download Instagram reels, videos, photos, and audio to your device. Save any reels video to your phone or computer and view it offline anytime.

It works right in your browser on any phone, tablet, or computer — no login, no app install. Just copy a public Instagram link, paste it in the box above, and download.

How it works

Three steps. Five seconds.

No software to install, no account to create. If you can copy a link, you can download.

1

Copy the link

Open Instagram, tap Share → Copy link on any public reel, video or photo post.

2

Paste it above

Drop the link into the box at the top of this page and hit Download.

3

Save the file

Preview the media, then tap download. It saves straight to your Downloads or gallery.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before you hit download.

Is InstaSave really free?

Yes — 100% free with unlimited downloads, no account and no hidden charges.

Do I need an Instagram account?

No. InstaSave only downloads public posts and never asks for your Instagram login, so your account is never at risk.

Can I download reels without a watermark?

Yes. Files are fetched from Instagram's own servers in their original form — no added watermark.

What can I download?

Public reels, video posts, photos, carousels, reel audio as MP3, profile pictures as displayed on Instagram, and public Facebook videos.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes — any mobile or desktop browser works: Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge. No app to install.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. Private content is never accessible to any downloader — any site claiming otherwise is lying.

Is downloading Instagram content legal?

Download only content you own or have permission to save, and keep downloads for personal, offline use. Respect the creator's copyright.

Where do downloaded files go?

On iPhone they save to the Files app or Photos; on Android to your Downloads folder or gallery; on PC to Downloads.

Do you store my links or downloads?

No. Links are processed in memory and not stored or shared with third parties.
