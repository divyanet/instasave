Download Instagram reels the smart way

Instagram doesn't offer a built-in "save video" button for reels posted by others — and screen recording kills quality. InstaSave fetches the original file from Instagram's content servers, so you get the same quality the creator uploaded, ready for offline viewing, editing or rewatching on a flight.

It works with every kind of public post: reels, single photos, video posts and carousels. Just make sure the account is public — private posts, stories and DMs are never accessible to any downloader, including ours, and any site claiming otherwise is lying.

A note on availability

Instagram aggressively blocks automated requests from data-center servers. Most of the time our fetch goes through instantly. During a block, instead of showing you a dead error page like other sites do, InstaSave still shows the reel's cover photo, title and author — and the video download unlocks automatically the moment Instagram allows it again. Honest beats broken.

Use it responsibly

Downloaded content belongs to its creator. Keep saves for personal, offline use, don't re-upload someone else's work as your own, and always credit the original creator if you share with permission. See our FAQ for the full guidance.

Never. InstaSave only works with public posts and never touches your account, so there's zero risk of a ban or hack.
