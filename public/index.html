Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the things people ask us most.

Is InstaSave really free?
Yes — 100% free, unlimited downloads, no account, no hidden paywall. If you ever see a site charging for this, walk away.
Do I need an Instagram account to use it?
Never. InstaSave only works with public posts and never touches your account, so there's zero risk of a ban or hack.
Will there be a watermark on my download?
No. Unlike screen recording or re-upload apps, we pull the original file from Instagram's servers — no added logos, ever.
What can I download with InstaSave?
Public reels, video posts, photos, carousels, reel audio as MP3, profile pictures in high resolution, and public Facebook videos. Seven dedicated tools, one site.
Can I download from private accounts?
No, and neither can any legitimate tool. Private content requires the account owner's login. Respect their privacy.
Can I download Instagram stories?
Honest answer: Instagram only serves stories to logged-in users, so no legitimate no-login tool can fetch them. Read our honest story guide for what actually works.
Does it work on iPhone?
Yes. Open this page in Safari, paste the link, download — the file lands in your Files app / Photos. Full steps: how-to guide.
Is downloading Instagram reels legal?
Save only content you own or have permission to keep, and use downloads for personal, offline viewing. Don't re-upload someone else's work as your own.
Sometimes the video button is missing — why?
Instagram temporarily blocks server-side video fetches. You'll still get the cover photo, title and author, and the MP4 unlocks automatically once the block lifts. Photo downloads are unaffected.
Do you store my links or downloads?
No. Links are processed in memory and never logged or stored. We set no tracking cookies — only your theme preference is saved, in your own browser.
How is InstaSave different from other downloaders?
No login, no watermark, unlimited free use, and honesty when Instagram rate-limits a fetch — we show you the cover, title and author instead of a fake loading spinner.
