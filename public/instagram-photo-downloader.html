Photo downloader FAQs

How do I download an Instagram photo in high resolution?
Copy the photo post's link (⋯ menu → Copy link), paste it into the box above and hit Download. You get the full-resolution JPG from Instagram's servers — far sharper than a screenshot.
Can I download all photos from an Instagram carousel?
Yes. Paste the carousel post's link and InstaSave lists every photo in it, each with its own high-resolution download button. Pick one or save them all.
Why do Instagram photos look blurry when I screenshot them?
A screenshot captures your screen's display, not the original image file. InstaSave fetches the actual uploaded file, so downloads are the full quality the photographer posted.
Can I download photos from a private account?
No — and neither can any legitimate tool. Instagram never exposes private-account content to third-party services. Respect the account owner's privacy.
Is it legal to download Instagram photos?
Keep downloads for personal use and only save photos you own or have permission to keep. Don't repost someone else's photo as your own — always credit the photographer.
How do I save Instagram photos on iPhone?
Copy the photo's link in the Instagram app, paste it into this page in Safari and tap Download. The JPG saves to your Files app — move it to Photos in one tap.
What format do photos download in?
JPG, in the highest resolution Instagram serves for the post — usually 1080px wide or more for recent uploads.
