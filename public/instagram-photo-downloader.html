How to download Instagram photos

Instagram shows photos beautifully but gives you no way to keep them. Long-pressing saves nothing, screenshots lose quality. InstaSave fetches the original image file from Instagram's servers:

  1. Copy the post link: tap ⋯ → Copy link on the photo post (or copy the URL on desktop).
  2. Paste it above and hit Download.
  3. Save each photo: single photos get one download button; carousels show every image with its own button.

Carousel posts: download every photo

Most downloaders only grab the first image of a carousel. InstaSave detects multi-photo posts and lists each image separately with individual download buttons — perfect for saving full photo dumps, before/after sets and infographics.

What quality will I get?

Photos download as JPG files at Instagram's served resolution — the same file your browser loads when you open the post, with no added watermark or compression from our side.

Can I download profile pictures too?

This tool is built for post links (instagram.com/p/…). Profile-photo downloads are a different feature and not currently supported — paste a post link instead.

A quick word on rights

Photos belong to the photographer or creator who posted them. Save them for personal use (wallpapers, mood boards, offline reference) and never repost without permission and credit. Details in the FAQ.
