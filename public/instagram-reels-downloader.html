How to download Instagram reels

Instagram lets you bookmark reels inside the app, but it won't let you keep the actual video file — that's where a reels downloader comes in. InstaSave grabs the original MP4 from Instagram's servers, so the quality matches what the creator uploaded — far better than a screen recording.

  1. Copy the reel link: in the Instagram app tap Share → Copy link under the reel (or copy the URL from your browser's address bar on desktop).
  2. Paste it above and hit Download.
  3. Save the MP4: preview the reel's cover, title and author, then tap the download button. On iPhone it lands in Files/Photos; on Android in Downloads; on PC wherever your browser saves files.

What quality will I get?

Reels download in their original uploaded resolution (typically 1080×1920) as an MP4 file — the same file Instagram streams to the app, minus the player chrome. There's no re-encoding on our side and no watermark stamped on top, unlike "repost" apps.

Why do some reels show only a preview?

Instagram rate-limits automated traffic from servers very aggressively. When a block is active, we can't pull the MP4 for a few minutes — but instead of failing silently, InstaSave still shows the reel's cover photo, caption title and author, and the video button reappears automatically once Instagram lifts the block. Photo posts are never affected. No other free downloader is this transparent about it.

Reels vs. posts vs. stories

This page handles reels (instagram.com/reel/…). For still images use our Instagram photo downloader. We deliberately don't touch stories, DMs or private accounts — anything claiming to download those is either a scam or asking for your password.

Is downloading reels legal?

Downloading for personal offline viewing (watching on a flight, studying an edit) is what this tool is for. Re-uploading someone else's reel as your own violates their copyright and can get your account struck. Always credit creators when you share their work with permission. More in the FAQ.
