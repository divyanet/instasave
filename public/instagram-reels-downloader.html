Reels downloader FAQs

How do I download Instagram reels without a watermark?
Copy the reel's link from Instagram, paste it into the box above and hit Download. Because we fetch the original file from Instagram's servers, the MP4 has no added watermark — unlike screen recordings or re-upload apps.
Can I download Instagram reels in high quality?
Yes. InstaSave automatically selects the highest-quality progressive MP4 stream Instagram serves for the reel — typically 1080p with the original soundtrack.
How do I download reels on iPhone?
In the Instagram app, tap the share icon (✈) on the reel and choose Copy Link. Open this page in Safari, paste the link, tap Download — the MP4 saves to your Files app or Photos.
Can I download reels from a private account?
No — and neither can any legitimate tool. Instagram never exposes private-account content to third-party services. Respect the account owner's privacy.
Is it legal to download Instagram reels?
Keep downloads for personal, offline viewing, and only save content you own or have permission to keep. Never re-upload someone else's reel as your own — credit the creator.
Can I download just the audio from a reel?
Yes. Paste the same reel link into our Instagram audio downloader and it converts the reel's soundtrack to an MP3 you can listen to offline or use in edits.
Why did the video download button not appear?
Instagram temporarily rate-limits automated video fetches from data-center servers. We still show the reel's cover photo, title and author, and the video unlocks automatically once the block lifts. Photo downloads are unaffected.
Can I download many reels at once?
InstaSave processes one link at a time to keep each fetch fast and reliable. Paste links one by one — each one takes only seconds.
