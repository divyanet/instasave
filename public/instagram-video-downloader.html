Can I download Instagram videos in high quality?
Yes. We automatically select the best-quality progressive MP4 stream Instagram provides for the post — typically up to 1080p with original audio.
Do downloaded videos include sound?
Yes. Videos download with their original audio track intact — AAC audio inside the MP4 container.

Instagram occasionally rate-limits automated video fetches from servers. We still show the cover photo, title and author, and the video unlocks automatically once the block lifts.
